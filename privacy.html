Privacy policy

Channel tools · last updated 8 November 2026

Who this covers

Channel tools is a private application used only by its owner, Marco Migozzi, on his own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It has no other users.

Data accessed

Through Google OAuth, with the owner's explicit consent, the application uses these YouTube scopes:

  • youtube.upload and youtube: to upload and manage the owner's own videos.
  • youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly: to read the statistics and analytics of the owner's own channels and videos.

It does not request the user's email, contacts, Gmail, Drive or any other Google data.

How data is used and stored

  • Statistics are stored in a private PostgreSQL database on a server the owner runs himself, and are used only to show him how his videos perform.
  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the same server and are never logged or published.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept for as long as the owner wants to track his channels. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions; the stored tokens then stop working and can be deleted from the server.
