Channel tools

Private tools of Marco Migozzi for his own video channels.

This application is a set of personal tools used by one person, Marco Migozzi, to manage his own channels on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok: Specie Curiosa, Cubopoli and Domani è tardi.

What it does

  • Uploads the videos Marco produces to his own YouTube channels.
  • Reads the public counters and the analytics of those same channels (views, likes, comments, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources) and stores them in a private database on Marco's own server, so he can see how his videos perform over time.

It is not offered to the public and has no other users. It never accesses channels or accounts that do not belong to Marco.
